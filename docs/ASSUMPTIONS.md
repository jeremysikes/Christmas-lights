# LightPlan Phase 1 — Assumptions

These were fixed before building. Each one is a place where a real house or a real product can differ from the model, so each is editable or flagged in the UI.

1. **Units and electrical context.** US feet and inches, 120 V North American residential circuits, USD. Product specs (watts, max strings end to end, prices) are typical retail values, not one brand. All of it lives in an editable JSON product library.
2. **Camera model.** Pinhole camera, square pixels, principal point at the image center, no lens-distortion correction. Phone main cameras correct distortion in software; 0.5x ultra-wide photos will measure less accurately.
3. **Focal length.** Taken from the photo's EXIF data when present (ignored when the EXIF image size shows the photo was cropped). Otherwise estimated from the perspective rectangle when the photo is angled enough. Otherwise assumed to be a phone main camera (26 mm equivalent), which caps roof and ground items at Low. Whatever the source, it is checked against the rectangles in the photo, and a mismatch is flagged with the measured value. Street View screenshots carry no EXIF and Street View zooms, so keep "Automatic" once a rectangle is marked; the "Street View" preset (90° field of view) only fits an unzoomed view.
4. **Geometry.** Walls are vertical planes and eaves are horizontal. A gable rake seen face-on lies in the wall plane, so it measures at true length and is used to estimate roof pitch. Roof slopes and the ground (walkways, driveways) are derived planes, computed from the wall's recovered camera pose, so receding rakes, ridges and walkway edges get real 3D lengths; they are very sensitive to tap error, which their confidence reflects. Trees and bushes traced on a wall stand in front of it, so their sizes are approximate and flagged; traced on a ground plane they are measured on a vertical plane through their base.
5. **Waste.** 10% is added to every linear run for waste, corners and drops to the outlet (editable). Strings are counted along each chain of connected runs (a string carries on around a corner), and only the strings that cover the true length are hung and counted as load; the rest are spares. Bush and tree counts use rules of thumb instead of a waste factor.
6. **Power.** Continuous load on a circuit is kept at or below 80% of the breaker rating (15 A = 1,440 W, 20 A = 1,920 W). Everything plugged into one outlet shares one receptacle, one timer and a chain of power stakes, all 15 A plug-in gear, so an outlet also carries at most 1,440 W whatever the breaker. Cords are sized on the standard lengths actually bought, by ampacity (capped at the 15 A plug) and a 3% voltage-drop limit. One timer or smart plug per outlet used; more than 1,250 W of incandescent load through one timer is flagged. Extra cords at an outlet use power stakes chained from the timer's socket. Cord distances between walls, roof and ground are worked out in 3D from the photo.
7. **Storage and files.** Autosave stays in this browser only (localStorage for the project, IndexedDB for the photo, every access wrapped in try/catch). When storage is blocked, a note says the plan will be lost on close and offers Save project; an autosave this version cannot read is kept as a backup to download; when another tab saves over the plan, autosave pauses until the user picks one. Photos are downscaled to 2048 px on the long side; the measurement math does not depend on resolution. Inside the claude.ai artifact viewer, exports go through the viewer's download prompt; as a standalone file, they are normal browser downloads. The PDF is generated in the browser because the artifact frame blocks the print dialog.
8. **Camera capture.** Uses the phone's own camera app through the file picker (`capture` attribute), because the artifact frame blocks live camera APIs.
9. **Out of scope for Phase 1.** Accounts, live pricing, Street View or aerial imagery, automatic edge detection. See `PHASE2_ROADMAP.md`.
10. **Not an inspection.** The power plan is a planning estimate, not an electrical inspection.
